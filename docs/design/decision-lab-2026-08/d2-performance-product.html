Decision D2 · owner comments 17, 18, 20, 22 · governs UIR-009, UIR-018, UIR-019

Are Dashboard and Career
one performance product?

Both routes read the same careerCoachReport and print the same verdict, the same ROI, and the same drawdown. The decision is whether that is one product shown twice, or two products that happen to share a calculation.

What exists today — verified 2026-08-10 against the seeded demo dataset

The same money answer, printed five times on one screen

On /dashboard with 250 tournaments loaded, the string $843.49 appears five times in the rendered text — wire tape, breadcrumb title, monument, and the "30-second answer" card — and ROI is printed three times. "Hold Current Stake" is rendered twice on the same viewport, once by MonumentCurve and once by VerdictGauge.

Then /career prints the same verdict a sixth time: "STAKE READINESS · HOLD CURRENT STAKE · 61/100 · ROI/LAST 20 306.7% · MAX DRAWDOWN 16.0 ABI · TOP BLOCKER: Range Compliance". Both pages call buildCareerCoachReport(); Dashboard renders it as VerdictGauge, Career as CareerCoachCard.

Career is also 15,912px tall, of which the unpaginated milestone timeline is 12,112px — 76% of the page. And it renders in hues that are not in tokens.css: sky, cyan and amber badges, where D13 purged amber system-wide.
