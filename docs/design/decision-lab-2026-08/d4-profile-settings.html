Decision D4 · owner comments 4, 5 · governs UIR-007

Does the hero block
become the settings home?

Data Vault is currently a first-class sidebar destination that a user visits roughly never, and the hero avatar at the bottom of the rail — the thing that looks clickable — is a static <div>.

What exists today — verified 2026-08-10

Everything D4 asks for already exists; none of it is where you'd look

Sidebar.tsx:46–54 renders the hero block as a plain div: an avatar circle with the first initial, the hero name, and the static label "Hero". It has no handler, no role, no keyboard affordance.

Meanwhile /data ("Data Vault") already contains exactly the D4 inventory: a Settings card with hero name (persisted to the IndexedDB settings table) and strategy profile (Baseline / Advanced Theory), the vault row census — 184,000 rows across 11 tables — export to a backup file, and restore with merge-or-replace behind a confirm dialog. It is pinned in the sidebar under a "Data" section heading of its own.

Navigation itself is the constraint. Below 768px the sidebar becomes a horizontally scrolling bottom bar; at 390px only 5 of the 10 nav items are visible without scrolling, and labels are hidden entirely — the user sees five unlabelled icons.

Scope check. The entire settings surface is two controls — hero name and strategy profile. SettingsCard.tsx is the only settings component in src/. There is no display preference, no theme (D2 forbids one), no notification setting.
Recoverability. Export/restore/replace are the app's only data-loss-adjacent actions and currently sit one click from the sidebar. Any move must not make recovery less findable.
