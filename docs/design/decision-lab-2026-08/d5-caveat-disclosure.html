Owner decision · 2026-08-10

D5 ratified: one shared chip → reason → “Why?” component, rendered only where earned. The permanent HonestyStrip is retired.

Decision D5 · owner comments 7, 13, 23 · governs UIR-012, UIR-016

Caveats become
progressive disclosure

The honesty boundary is the brand and is not negotiable. The problem is not that the app says what it can't do — it's that the saying is unevenly distributed: saturated where the math is safest, absent where the denominator is weakest.

What exists today — verified 2026-08-10, text census on the seeded demo dataset

Leaks says it eight times; Dashboard never says it at all

A token census of each page's rendered text: on /leaks, "rule-based" appears 8 times, "no solver" 7 times and "directional" 3 times — inside only 3,619 characters, roughly one honesty token per 200 characters. Leak #1 alone carries five badges, two of which (Rule-based Rule-based, no EV) say the same thing twice, plus a prose caveat repeated near-verbatim on leaks #2 and #3.

On /dashboard the same census returns zero — no "rule-based", no "not graded", no sample note — while the page prints ROI +306.7% and ITM 26.0%. That ITM denominator is 65 of 250 tournaments: 185 have no recorded finish. The page with the least honest denominator carries the least honesty copy.

Coach's Note sits in between: "not graded" 5×, the local-only claim 3×, plus a permanent three-cell HonestyStrip that renders identically on every visit whether or not anything was refused.

Consequence for copy. "facing 3-bets, all-ins, multiway BB defense" is listed as the reason on screen, but none of those scenarios exist in this dataset — the stated reason does not match the actual cause.
