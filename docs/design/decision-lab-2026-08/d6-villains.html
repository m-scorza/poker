Decision D6 · owner comment 32 · governs UIR-023

Does Villains
earn a route?

After auto-archetypes were parked in 39f56d5, the page became a bare observed-stats table. The question is whether a repeat-opponent workflow justifies a first-class destination, or whether opponent context belongs inside Hands and Sessions.

What exists today — verified 2026-08-10

Seven columns, two of them structurally zero

VillainsPage.tsx renders a search box, a player count, and a 7-column table (Player · Hands · VPIP · PFR · AF · Limp% · Gap) capped at 100 rows, plus a click-through detail panel with free-text notes and ten predefined tags. That is the whole route — 368 lines, no encounter history, no session comparison, no sample-confidence indicator, no sort.

On the demo dataset the table renders 10 players whose stats are statistically indistinguishable: VPIP spans 13.0–14.1%, PFR 7.9–8.8%, AF 1.41–1.73 — and Limp% is 0.0% and 3-bet% is 0.0% for every single player. Two of the seven columns carry no information at all, and the "Gap" column ranges 5.0–5.7 across the entire field. A user cannot tell "Loose Leo" from "Nit Nora" from this screen.

The page also has no empty-state design beyond one sentence, and at 390px it overflows horizontally (scrollWidth 461 vs 390).
